Why These Metric Targets?

Hit Rate > 70%

Best baseline (BM25+Vector) = 66%. +4% proves graph signal value. GraphRAG literature (MS 2024) reports +5-15% over dense-only.

MRR > 0.50

Baseline MRR ~0.48. Modest but verifiable; aligned with BEIR benchmark norms for domain-specific Vietnamese retrieval.

EM > 30%, F1 > 50%

Matches UIT-ViQuAD 2.0 leaderboard: top XLM-R systems achieve ~35% EM, ~65% F1.

Current: Hit Rate = 64.67% (gap to 70% targeted W8-10), MRR = ~0.52 (target met).
